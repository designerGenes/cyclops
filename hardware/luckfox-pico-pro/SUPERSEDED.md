# SUPERSEDED — v0 "top-hood" enclosure

The `luckfox_enclosure_*` design generated by `enclosure_geometry.py` +
`generate_case.py` (and its `out/stl/` outputs) is **superseded**.

**Superseded by:** [`v1_1/src/luckfox_pill_enclosure.py`](v1_1/src/luckfox_pill_enclosure.py)
— the "fat pill" wall-camera enclosure, generator version 1.1. Read `v1_1/DESIGN.md`.

| Path | Contents |

## Why v0 was retired

Per `v1_1/DESIGN.md`: base and cover met in a flat butt joint with no screws,
clips, lip or overlap, so nothing held them together; the gasket groove left
0.2 mm side walls and bridged an open port; the hood pins did not retain; the
hood plate sat 8 mm in front of the camera aperture and blocked the field of
view; and there were no standoffs, camera mount or wall-mount features. The
replacement uses a screw closure, an O-ring cord seal, a printed board sled and
a wall bracket.
